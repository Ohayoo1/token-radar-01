        What this board does not claim

        • It does not predict whether or when a protocol issues a token. It prices the record as it stands.
        • “Records no token” is DefiLlama's record, not proof: a protocol on the board may have a token its record does not carry.
        • The band on each row is the interquartile range of its category's multiples, not a forecast of a price.
